Math, Comparison operator , and logic in python

--- What are the operator

operator are symbols that perform operations on values. Think of them as the "verbs" of programming - They make things happen!

allredy know most operators from math class:

> Calculate: + , - , * , /
> Compare: > , < , ==
> Combine: and, or , not

Arithmatic operators

These work just like calculates:

# Basic math
print(10 + 3)   # 13 - Addition
print(10 - 3)   # 7  - Subtraction
print(10 * 3)   # 30 - Multiplication
print(10 / 3)   # 3.333... - Division (always gives float)

# Special operators
print(10 // 3)  # 3  - Floor division (rounds down)
print(10 % 3)   # 1  - Modulo (remainder)
print(10 ** 3)  # 1000 - Exponent (power)
